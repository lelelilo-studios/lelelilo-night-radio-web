    Night Radio

    What
    A showcase of the ElevenLabs API, room by room: speech in many voices and languages, acted lines, whole scenes, new voices from a description, changed and cleaned voices, sound effects, music, transcripts (recorded and live), alignment, dubbing and a voice agent. Every sound in the rooms was made by the API once, and kept.
    Each room
    An animation that follows its sound, a play bar, and a care tag with exactly what went in (text, voices, settings), what came back, the API call, and what it cost.
    Cost
    Measured from the account's credit balance before and after each room, on a Creator plan. The whole station cost less than a cup of coffee.
    Plays on
    Phones and computers. The animations need WebGPU: Chrome on Android and computers, Edge, Safari 26+ on iPhone, iPad and Mac. Sound starts when you press play.
    Care
    Best after midnight, with headphones. Do not tumble dry. More from the studio on lelelilo.com.
